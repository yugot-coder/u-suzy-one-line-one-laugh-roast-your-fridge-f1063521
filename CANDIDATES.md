# Candidates — One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge

Competition: `u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521`
Judge criteria (most important first): roasts a fridge or phone; specific,
recognisable detail; twist / surprise / double meaning paying off in the last
few words; reads cleanly aloud as one line; feels fresh, not a worn-out joke.

## The 10 candidates

1. (112 chars) "My fridge hums all night like it's gossiping about me, then acts innocent when I open it and the light comes on."
   — Personification of the hum is nice, but the light gag is the most worn-out fridge joke there is.

2. (111 chars) "My fridge's crisper drawer isn't for keeping vegetables fresh — it's where lettuce goes to file for retirement."
   — Specific detail (crisper drawer), decent twist, but "retirement" lands soft and the setup is long.

3. (114 chars) "My fridge beeps if the door stays open five seconds, like it's tattling on me for thinking about a midnight snack."
   — Very specific (door alarm), relatable, but the twist is just "tattling", a mild rephrase of the beep.

4. (129 chars) "The ice maker in my fridge sounds like it's chewing gravel at 3 a.m., then delivers exactly one cube like it's doing me a favour."
   — Strong specific detail and a good second beat, but two jokes in one line dilute the payoff; a bit long.

5. (106 chars) "My fridge has a light that only works when I'm not looking for anything, and a smell that finds me anyway."
   — Reads well, but the light is a cliché and the smell line is generic.

6. (115 chars) "My fridge keeps everything cold except its attitude — it hums louder every time I reach for the last slice of cake."
   — "Attitude" is a generic insult; the hum detail is reused better in #1.

7. (110 chars) "My fridge's freezer has food older than some of my friendships, labelled in handwriting I no longer recognise."
   — Specific and visual, twist is the unrecognised handwriting, but the payoff is more sad than funny.

8. (108 chars) "My phone's battery dies at 2% like a drama queen, then stays alive for an hour on 1% just to watch me panic."
   — Best phone candidate: specific (2% / 1%), strong twist, but "drama queen" is a tired phrase and phone-battery jokes are everywhere.

9. (109 chars) "My fridge hums a different note when it's empty, like it's singing the blues about the groceries I never buy."
   — Charming image, but "singing the blues" is a pun everyone has heard, and the twist is predictable.

10. (122 chars) "The magnets on my fridge hold up takeaway menus for restaurants that closed years ago — it's a museum with a light inside."
    — CHOSEN. Specific, instantly recognisable detail (fridge magnets holding dead takeaway menus); the twist lands in the last five words, reframing the whole appliance as a museum; fresh — not a hum/light/diet joke; reads cleanly aloud in one breath.

## Why #10 wins

- Criterion 1: unmistakably a fridge roast.
- Criterion 2: the magnet-held takeaway menu is a concrete detail almost every fridge owner recognises, not a generic insult.
- Criterion 3: the payoff ("a museum with a light inside") reframes the fridge entirely and lands at the very end.
- Criterion 4: one smooth sentence, no setup needing a second line.
- Criterion 5: avoids the three most worn-out fridge jokes (the hum, the light, the diet/leftovers).
